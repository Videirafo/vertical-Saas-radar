Glossary

Terms used by the consequence-scoring and briefing surfaces.

Consequence score

A deterministic 0–100 prioritization score derived from impact dimensions, concrete change terms and a promotional-language penalty.

Meaningful signal

A normalized change that crosses the current consequence threshold and is promoted into the main weekly brief.

Watchlist

A lower-consequence or lower-confidence change that remains visible but does not currently justify the same level of attention.

Hype indicator

A lightweight heuristic for promotional wording. It lowers confidence but never replaces source verification.

Evidence URL

The canonical public page used to verify a release, changelog entry or product change.
